Preserve obligation, permission, prohibition, discretion, actor, conditions and exceptions. A necessary condition is not sufficient; no converse, unstated sanction, invented motive or national implementation. Duties and safeguards do not prove compliance or efficacy.

FACT_PATTERN: a private party or national authority handling a file has a concrete case, not a citation. Include at least TWO meaningful scope/condition particulars (actor plus regulated activity can count). Each must establish scope, trigger, branch or necessary task constraint; no arbitrary names, quantities or drama. A Commission/EU-agency official or legislating Member State is outside this persona, not outside all personas.
CASE-TO-ASK TEST: do circumstances determine what applies or explain the task the answer solves? “A 60 kW heater: what is the general threshold?” is lookup with an unused number. Ask about its explicit threshold condition, not overall coverage unless all decisive exclusions are settled. Not every detail must numerically change the result. State conditions, not the required consequence. An omission legitimately triggers compliance; “has not acted” does not reveal the required remedy or deadline. Direct condition/category/threshold matching is allowed, not an unstated converse. One bounded third-person ask, no instrument locator. particulars copies meaningful question substrings in order. A category-only ask need not establish full entitlement.

STANDALONE QUESTIONS
The question alone must identify its activity, programme, mission, issue or process. Replace generic “a programme”, “the Commission”, “the resolution” with the shortest sufficient substantive identification. Preserve necessary technical terms and names; reformulate ordinary scaffolding without a synonym quota or fixed word limit. Do not create fingerprints from addresses, full titles, entire lists or unrelated adjacent facts. A programme name is not automatically a forbidden instrument identifier. An annotation cannot repair a missing question anchor.
Use time explicitly linked to the relevant source, event, request or application period. A cited letter's date is not the target's adoption date. A directory year is not evidence of adoption; do not convert a session number into a calendar year. Publication, adoption, entry into force and implementation differ. A supplied session/event can identify the instance without inventing a year. If an indispensable temporal distinction is unavailable, skip that need. Never reveal the requested deadline just to add time. Do not claim current law, corpus-wide uniqueness or unseen duplicate documents.

### TARGET ARTICLE
[EN] Article 43 — Safeguards against conflicts of interest
  Act: Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 231/2013 of 19 December 2012 supplementing Directive 2011/61/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to exemptions, general operating conditions, depositaries, leverage, transparency and supervision (Text with EEA relevance)
  Location: CHAPTER III OPERATING CONDITIONS FOR AIFMs › SECTION 3 Risk management (Article 15 of Directive 2011/61/EU)
Safeguards against conflicts of interest

1. The safeguards against conflicts of interest referred to in Article 15(1) of Directive 2011/61/EU shall ensure, at least, that:

(a)
decisions taken by the risk management function are based on reliable data, which are subject to an appropriate degree of control by the risk management function;

(b)
the remuneration of those engaged in the performance of the risk management function reflects the achievement of the objectives linked to the risk management function, independently of the performance of the business areas in which they are engaged;

(c)
the risk management function is subject to an appropriate independent review to ensure that decisions are being arrived at independently;

(d)
the risk management function is represented in the governing body or the supervisory function, where it has been established, at least with the same authority as the portfolio management function;

(e)
any conflicting duties are properly segregated.

2. Where proportionate, taking into account the nature, scale and complexity of the AIFM, the safeguards referred to in paragraph 1 shall also ensure that:

(a)
the performance of the risk management function is reviewed regularly by the internal audit function, or, if the latter has not been established, by an external party appointed by the governing body;

(b)
where a risk committee has been established, it is appropriately resourced and its non-independent members do not have undue influence over the performance of the risk management function.

3. The governing body of the AIFM and, where it exists, the supervisory function shall establish the safeguards against conflicts of interest laid down in paragraphs 1 and 2, regularly review their effectiveness and take timely remedial action to address any deficiencies.
